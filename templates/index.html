How the System Works

02
📊

Workload Signals

03
🤖

ML Forecast

Predicts future CPU utilization from learned workload patterns.

04
🧮

Capacity Decision

Converts the predicted peak into required cloud instances.

05
⚡

Predictive Action

Prepares, executes or releases capacity through the simulator.
